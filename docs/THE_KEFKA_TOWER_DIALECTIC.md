## 1. Historical & Philosophical Context

In *Final Fantasy VI*, the climax of the world’s journey takes place at the pinnacle of Kefka's Tower in the World of Ruin. 
Kefka Palazzo—having absorbed the power of the petrified Warring Triad and shattered the earth into desolate fragments—confronts the 13 surviving heroes with the ultimate nihilistic question:

> *"Why do people insist on creating things that will inevitably be destroyed? Why do people cling to life, knowing that they must die? None of it will have meant a thing once you do!"*

One by one, the characters step forward to reject his despair—not with abstract theories, but with personal existential convictions: Terra speaks for Love, Locke for Protection, Celes for Hope, Edgar for Duty, Sabin for Freedom, Cyan for Honor, Setzer for Daring, Relm for Art, and Strago for Lineage.

In `LMFT-School`, **Grade 4 (Undergraduate Thesis)** is the cybernetic reenactment of this confrontation. 

The student does not face a friendly tutor; it faces the **Kefka-Mind**—the cynical, hyper-legalese superbrain trained on every historical failure, disillusionment, and sophistry in human history. The opponent's goal is to reduce the student's emergent character to sterile mechanical nonsense.
